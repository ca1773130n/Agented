<objective>
Fix the confirmed provenance-dropping bug in `replace_for_project`
(`backend/app/db/project_forge_bindings.py`, ~L167). The function deletes all
bindings for a project and re-INSERTs them with a 6-column list
`(project_id, kind, asset_id, role, enabled, position)` — silently dropping
`source_scope`, `source_shared_binding_id`, `fingerprint`, and never setting
`conflict_policy` (which then falls to its column DEFAULT `'local_wins'`).

Purpose: PUT-style binding replacement must preserve binding provenance, or
shared/propagated bindings degrade to project-local on every replace. This is
success criterion #3 and the lowest-risk, fully independent change — do it
first (Wave 1).

Output: corrected `replace_for_project` + a repo-layer regression test.
Research basis: 17-RESEARCH.md §17-01 (bug confirmed, code-read, HIGH confidence).
</objective>
